# Shams Roj Organization Website Project Brief

## 1. Project Overview

**Project Name:** Shams Roj Organization Official Website

**Purpose:** To create a modern, user-friendly, and responsive website for the Shams Roj Organization. The website will serve as a central hub for information about the organization's mission, objectives, founders, and guiding principles, aiming to increase transparency, attract support, and engage with the community.

**Target Audience:** Potential donors, volunteers, community members, partners, and the general public interested in the organization's work.

## 2. Organization Background

Shams Roj is a non-profit entity dedicated to fostering community development and upholding principles of justice and equality. The organization focuses on various humanitarian and developmental activities, as outlined in its objectives and guided by a strong ethical framework.

## 3. Website Features and Requirements

### 3.1. General Structure
The website should include, but not be limited to, the following main sections:
*   **Home:** An engaging landing page introducing Shams Roj, its mission, and a call to action.
*   **About Us:** Detailed information about the organization, its history, and its vision.
*   **Our Objectives:** A dedicated section outlining the organization's goals and main activities.
*   **Our Founders:** A section introducing the key individuals who founded and lead the organization.
*   **Guiding Principles:** A comprehensive overview of the ethical and guiding principles that govern the organization's operations.
*   **Contact Us:** Information on how to get in touch with Shams Roj, including a contact form or relevant details.

### 3.2. Content Integration (from provided documents)

#### **A. Objectives and Main Activities (from "الاهدافوالمؤسسين.pdf")**

The website must clearly present the following objectives and main activities:
1.  **Capacity Building:** Developing life skills to foster community growth.
2.  **Education:** Establishing educational centers and kindergartens across all stages.
3.  **Healthcare:** Providing medical care for vulnerable groups and improving health standards by establishing medical centers.
4.  **Social Support:** Addressing social issues and providing psychosocial support (PSS).
5.  **Volunteering:** Organizing volunteer campaigns and training programs.

#### **B. Members and Founders (from "الاهدافوالمؤسسين.pdf")**

#### **C. Guiding and Ethical Principles (from "المبادئالتوجيهيةوالأخلاقيةلمنظمةشمسروج.pdf")**

The website should dedicate a section to the organization's guiding and ethical principles, covering:
*   **Core Purpose and Scope:** Emphasizing integrity and professional competence for all associated personnel.
*   **Core Values:** Human Dignity and Diversity, Zero Tolerance (against violence, abuse, exploitation), Organizational Cohesion, and Solidarity and Justice.
*   **Prohibited Actions:** Clearly stating actions that are strictly forbidden (e.g., fraud, sexual exploitation, bullying, substance abuse).
*   **Reporting and Accountability:** Explaining the duty to report violations and the consequences of non-compliance.

### 3.3. Design Considerations
*   **Modern and Clean Aesthetic:** The design should be contemporary, professional, and easy to navigate.
*   **User-Friendly Interface:** Intuitive layout and clear calls to action.
*   **Responsive Design:** The website must be fully responsive and optimized for viewing on all devices (desktops, tablets, mobile phones).
*   **Branding:** Incorporate any existing branding elements (logo, color scheme) if provided. (Note: No branding elements were provided in the PDFs, so a default professional scheme can be used initially).

## 4. Technical Stack

The website will be built using the following technologies:
*   **Frontend Framework:** React.js
*   **Build Tool:** Vite
*   **Language:** JavaScript
*   **Styling:** CSS
